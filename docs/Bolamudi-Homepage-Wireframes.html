Annotations

01 Hero holds one idea only

Content occupies columns 1–7; columns 8–12 stay empty so the photograph reads. Height capped at 820px — an uncapped 100vh hero is a wall of nothing on a 27" display. H1 is the LCP element, not the image.

02 Vertical rules, not cards

Hairline dividers make the strip read as a technical spec sheet. Four items, not six — six creates a cramped row. Remaining two services appear in full at 04.

03 Problem before solution

Contains zero claims about Bolamudi, so nothing here is challengeable. Specificity is the credential. The 12px gold rule on the image ties the section to the brand without adding colour.

04 Six cards = breadth of practice

Whole card is one anchor. Hover changes border to gold and slides the arrow 4px. No lift, no shadow, no scale — restraint is the institutional signal.

05 First dark break at ~35% depth

Method substitutes for a track record. The connecting rule draws left-to-right once — the site's single signature animation. Alternating above/below is CSS only; DOM order stays 01→07.

06 Geography as evidence

List the districts you have actually worked in, not all 31. A short honest list outperforms a full map, which invites "all of them?". Text list must accompany the SVG for keyboard and mobile.

07 The highest-value section

Depth no competitor publishes, and the strongest organic-search asset available. No accordions — collapsing this hides the very depth it exists to show, and weakens indexing. The gated guide is the only gate on the site.

08 Deliberately quieter cards

Tinted fill, no icons, no hover. Non-interactive elements must not look clickable. The investor card earns equal weight because diligence work is the easiest first mandate to win.

09 Rows, not cards

Rows read as a considered argument; cards read as marketing. Every point is a fact or a method — never a quality. "We work only in Karnataka" persuades; "focused" does not.

10 The credibility anchor

In a relationship business, in one state, as a new entity, the promoter's 20 years is the entire asset. Anonymity here is what a shell company looks like — Arup, AECOM and Jacobs all name leadership. Fallback structure exists in the spec if overruled.

11 Pre-empts the liaison objection

Every institutional buyer with FCPA/UKBA exposure is thinking about it. Plain layout, fade only, no motion — this section must feel matter-of-fact.

12 Three real articles or none

Placeholder posts do more damage than an absent section. Cut to phase 2 if genuine content is not ready at launch.

13 Objections plus long-tail search

Sticky left column keeps context while the accordion runs long. FAQPage JSON-LD generated from the same data source that renders the accordion, so they cannot drift.

14 Eight fields on purpose

Against conversion orthodoxy, and correct here: a single senior practice cannot process volume, and a bare-email lead cannot be triaged. The two selects let the first reply be intelligent. Success replaces the form in place — no thank-you page.

15 Registration numbers in the footer

CIN and GSTIN are a disproportionately strong legitimacy signal for a new entity. Cheap to add, and the first thing a diligence-minded visitor looks for.

RAIL Section progress, ≥1440px only

A 9,600px page needs to feel navigable rather than endless. aria-hidden — it duplicates the nav. Hidden below 1440px where there is no spare margin.

Mobile annotations

M1 Hero height is auto, never 100vh

Mobile browser chrome makes 100vh unreliable and pushes the CTA below the fold. 72px vertical padding, H1 at 36px, CTAs stacked full width.

M2 Strip stays 2×2

Stacking four items to a single column produces four screens of two words each. 2×2 keeps it scannable in one viewport.

M3 Image leads the section

Text-then-image inverts on mobile so the section opens with a visual anchor. The gold rule moves from the left edge to a 5px top border.

M5 Timeline goes vertical

Horizontal-scroll timelines are consistently ignored on mobile because the scroll affordance is invisible. Vertical is the correct pattern for sequences.

M6 Text list replaces hover

Tooltips do not exist on touch. The district list must be visible content, not hover-only information — otherwise mobile users lose the section's entire proof value.

M10 Portrait constrained to 320px

A full-bleed face at 390px is overwhelming and reads as a personal site. Left-aligned and capped keeps it professional.

M14 Native selects, 48px inputs

Custom dropdowns are a recurring accessibility failure and native pickers are better on touch. Submit is 44px minimum. Phone number sits directly below the form as a fallback path.

M15 Footer columns become accordions

Otherwise the footer is 900px tall. Registration numbers stay always-visible.

BAR Sticky CTA, <768px

Appears after the hero, hides when the contact section is in view. A large share of Indian B2B mobile visitors want to call rather than fill a form. Page needs matching bottom padding plus safe-area inset so the bar never covers the footer.
