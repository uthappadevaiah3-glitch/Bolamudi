Land Aggregation

Contiguous, encumbrance-free, connectivity-viable land — assembled parcel by parcel and handed over in a condition that survives lender diligence.

Request a land feasibility read
The problem

Written in the buyer's language and naming specific failure modes. Generic problem statements signal you have never done this.

A 300 MW solar project needs roughly 1,200 to 1,500 acres. In Karnataka that is almost never one seller. It is 80 to 200 khatedars across four or five villages, with fragmented holdings, undivided family shares, minors' interests, tenancy exposure, grant and inam restrictions, and RTC entries that do not match who is actually farming the land.

Most developers discover this in the wrong order. Parcels are tied up on the strength of a broker's assurance, advances are paid, and the title problem surfaces during lender due diligence — eight weeks before financial close, when the cost of a gap in the chain is the entire drawdown schedule.

The second failure is geometric. Parcels get bought where they are available, not where the array needs them, and the project ends up with a polygon it cannot build on efficiently or evacuate from economically.

  • A 30-year title flow with an unresolved partition or an unrecorded mutation
  • Hold-out parcels sitting inside the array boundary, discovered after 80% acquisition
  • Land tied up at a price set before anyone benchmarked guidance value
  • Section 109 permission or conversion started after registration instead of before
  • A land file that a lender's counsel cannot audit
The solution

We assemble contiguous, encumbrance-free, connectivity-viable land and hand it over in a condition that survives lender diligence. Title is verified before money moves, not after.

One register, from first contactEvery parcel enters the register on day one and stays in it through registration, khata and mutation.

The process

Eight stages. Statute references shown as chips — the device that makes the page feel produced by people who handle these documents.

01

Site screening & terrain assessment

Polygon shortlisting against resource, slope, evacuation distance, forest and wildlife buffers, water bodies, habitations and existing infrastructure.

02

Survey number mapping

Village cadastral overlay; Bhoomi RTC pull for every survey number in the polygon; tippan / Mojini sketch reconciled to ground.

Bhoomi RTC · Mojini
03

Title due diligence

Title chain, encumbrance certificate, mutation register chain, partition status, minors' interest, PTCL / inam / grant restrictions and tenancy exposure. Conducted with empanelled counsel.

30-year flow
04

Owner engagement & rate discovery

Village-level engagement, guidance value versus prevailing market benchmarking, consent mapping, and early identification of hold-out risk inside the array boundary.

05

Agreement execution

Registered Agreement to Sale or lease deed, power of attorney where appropriate, advance schedule tied to documentary milestones.

06

Statutory pathway

Section 109 permission where the acquirer is a company — threshold raised to four hectares under the 2025 amendment; conversion via the Deputy Commissioner or the automatic conversion route available to renewable energy projects under the 2025 Land Revenue Rules.

KLR Act s.109 · LR Rules 2025
07

Registration & mutation

Sub-registrar execution, khata transfer, mutation into the SPV's name, and updated RTC reflecting the project entity.

08

Handover

Consolidated land dossier issued to developer and lender in audit format.

Deliverables

What you receive, named.

Physical artefacts, not activity descriptions. This is the section that converts.

Spatial

  • Village-wise project polygon with survey-number-level GIS layer
  • Array-boundary overlay — acquired, consented, in-progress, at-risk

Register & diligence

XLSX / PDF
  • Parcel register — survey no., extent, khatedar, RTC, encumbrance, consent, execution status
  • 30-year title opinion pack per parcel
  • Risk log for restricted, disputed and hold-out parcels

Executed documents

PDF · lender format
  • Registered Agreements to Sale / lease deeds
  • Section 109 permission order
  • Conversion order or deemed-conversion acknowledgement
  • Registered sale deeds, khata certificates, mutated RTCs in SPV name
  • Land Due Diligence Handover File
Applicability

How land aggregation differs by asset class.

Solar

Contiguity is the binding constraint, not rate. 4–5 acres per MW in one block.

Wind

Crane paths and access roads across owners with no turbine on their land.

Hybrid

Aggregate against the combined layout from the start, not solved twice.

Pumped Storage

Revenue, forest and private holdings on three different legal tracks.
